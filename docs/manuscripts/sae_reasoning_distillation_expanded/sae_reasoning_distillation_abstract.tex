\begin{abstract}
Longer teacher traces do not necessarily provide better supervision for smaller language models.
We study \textbf{SAE-guided reasoning distillation}: identify length-associated features in a shared sparse autoencoder using within-problem contrasts between short and long answer-correct traces, steer teacher generation, and distill the resulting outputs.
In an exploratory GSM8K study with a Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct teacher and Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct student, selected short-feature enhancement reduces teacher output length by 13.27\%.
Students trained on these outputs generate approximately 11\% fewer tokens than students trained on unmodified outputs.
Accuracy differences remain statistically inconclusive under equal-example and approximately equal-target-token budgets.
The results support feature-guided length control and its downstream transfer, without establishing improved student accuracy.
\end{abstract}
