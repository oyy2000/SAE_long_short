\begin{abstract}
Chain-of-thought distillation trains smaller models over teacher-generated reasoning, but long traces may contain redundant supervision.
Length-based selection filters completed traces without directly controlling how teachers generate them.
In this paper, we propose \textbf{SAE-guided reasoning distillation}, a feature-level intervention framework for producing concise teaching traces.
Given short and long correct solutions from the same teacher for the same problems, our method learns a shared sparse autoencoder, identifies distinct latent features, and encourages short-associated while suppressing long-associated directions during generation.
